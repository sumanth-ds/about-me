FAQ

Questions about this client project

Who is Mr. Mutukuru Rathnam?

Mr. Muthukuru Rathnam is one of the top 100 most effective Competitive English teaching faculties in India. Born in poverty in Kadapa district, Andhra Pradesh, he overcame immense challenges to become a marvellous mentor to thousands of students across AP, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Delhi, and Mumbai.

What was the client requirement?

The client needed a professional portfolio website to showcase his teaching journey, awards, media coverage, and provide a way for students, collaborators, and event organizers to reach him. The site had to be mobile-friendly, fast, and easy to maintain.

What technologies were used?

The website is built with React and deployed on Vercel for fast, global CDN delivery. It features responsive CSS, smooth scroll navigation, and a contact form with email integration.

Is this a live production project?

Yes. This is a live production project deployed at rathnam.vercel.app. It is actively used by the client to share his profile, achievements, and contact information with students and event organizers.
