FAQ

Questions about this client project

What is Divya Atelier?

Divya Atelier is a premium clothing showroom offering a wide range of clothing — women's wear (sarees, kurtis, lehengas), men's wear (shirts, suits, traditional), kids wear, school uniforms, accessories, and seasonal collections.

How does WhatsApp ordering work?

Customers can browse products on the website and tap the WhatsApp button to directly message the showroom with their product selection. This enables instant ordering, size inquiries, and personalized assistance without needing a complex cart/checkout system.

What product categories are available?

The platform covers 6 main categories: Women's Wear, Men's Wear, Kids Wear, School Uniforms, Accessories, and Seasonal Collections. Each category has multiple sub-categories with detailed product listings.

Is this a live production project?

Yes. This is a live production project deployed at divya-drab-beta.vercel.app. It is actively used by the client to showcase their clothing catalog and receive orders via WhatsApp.
